# 🏛️ AgriTrust National Agricultural Registry: Institutional Governance Charter

*Last Updated: March 31, 2026 | Document ID: AT-GOV-2026-V4*

## 1. Preamble & Mandate
AgriTrust is the authoritative digital twin for the national agricultural trade sector of Zimbabwe. By accessing the Command Center, Market Terminal, or Field Agent Bridge, all stakeholders ("Users") enter into a legally binding operational contract governed by the National Escrow Registry.

---

## 2. Professional Conduct & AI Integrity
The platform utilizes an **AI Price Oracle** and **Regional Yield Telemetry**. Users must acknowledge that:
*   **Neutrality:** Automated price deviations exceeding 20% from the National Commodity Index will trigger a Mandatory Audit.
*   **Non-Interference:** Manipulating scraping bots, market trend data, or institutional risk scores is a Tier-1 security violation.

## 3. The National Escrow Protocol
*   **Funds Commitment:** Buyers must securely commit 100% of the trade value to the multisig escrow vault before any production movement.
*   **Verifiable Fulfillment:** Producers must provide a 'Grade-A' certificate or equivalent regional node verification before escrow release.
*   **Irreversible Settlement:** Once a 'Release Handshake' is executed, the transaction is finalized. Reversals are only permitted during the 'Dispute Adjudication' phase by an authorized lead administrator.

## 4. Account Suspension & Absolute Revocation
AgriTrust maintains a zero-tolerance policy for system-wide trust erosion. Access is a privilege, not a right.

### 4.1 Tiered Disciplinary Triggers
*   **Trust Index Breach:** Any account whose Trust Score falls below the **40% Critical Floor** is subject to immediate algorithmic suspension.
*   **Synthetic Listing Fraud:** Falsifying inventory, grade certificates, or moisture levels results in permanent identity revocation and police referral.
*   **Identity Falsification:** Mismatches between the National ID Registry and platform biometrics result in an automatic financial freeze.
*   **Escrow Collusion:** Colluding with field agents to release funds without physical delivery leads to immediate platform blacklisting.

## 5. Dispute Resolution & Finality
In the event of a commercial dispute, the **Regional Adjudication Hub** serves as the primary arbiter. 
1.  **Direct Settlement:** Parties are encouraged to negotiate via the Direct Settlement offering system.
2.  **Institutional Adjudication:** If no agreement is reached, an AgriTrust Lead Agent will provide a final, binding decision within 24 hours.

## 6. Data Governance & Privacy
AgriTrust is the custodian of sensitive agricultural and financial metadata.
*   **Encryption:** All identity data (National ID, Phone) is encrypted-at-rest.
*   **Auditability:** Every keystroke and state-change within the Command Center is logged in the **Persistent Audit Ledger**.
