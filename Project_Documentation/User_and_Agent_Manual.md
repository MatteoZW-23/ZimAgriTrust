# AgriTrust General User Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to AgriTrust. This platform bridges the gap between Zimbabwean farmers and national procurement structures.

### For Farmers
* **Enroll**: Verify your identity through the national system. 
* **List**: Create open supply listings (e.g. 50 Tonnes of Wheat).
* **Escrow**: Rest assured that your capital is held securely in escrow while logistics delivers your cargo. 

### For Buyers
* **Discover**: Access audited agricultural supply instantly. 
* **Bid**: Propose deals and negotiate terms directly with verified farmers.
* **Release**: Once quality is confirmed by agents or delivery is received, release funds to complete the contract.
# AgriTrust Field Agent Operations Guide

## Overview
Field Agents are the backbone of AgriTrust's trust and verification system. This guide covers standard operating procedures.

### Core Responsibilities
1. **Farm Audits**: Physically visit farms and verify acreage, crop type, and expected yield.
2. **Quality Assurance**: Inspect produce before it enters the logistics chain or escrow matching.
3. **Dispute Resolution**: Serve as neutral arbiters for quality or delivery disputes between farmers and buyers.

### Dashboard Usage
1. Log into the `National Market Sync` portal via the Agent URL.
2. Monitor **Activity History** and **Audit Queue**.
3. Clear pending verifications for incoming listings.

### Dispute Escrow Procedures
* In case of disputes, propose a settlement (e.g. 50% refund for Grade B quality instead of A).
* Once both parties accept, or HQ overrides, the escrow is released.
