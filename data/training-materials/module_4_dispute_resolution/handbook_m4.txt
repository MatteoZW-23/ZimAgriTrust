ZimAgritrust Module 4: Dispute Resolution
====================================

This handbook covers the protocols for investigating disagreements between buyers and farmers, with a focus on the Negotiation Hub and conversation encryption.

4.1 The Negotiation Hub & Encryption
------------------------------------

# 🔐 NEGOTIATION HUB – Agent Role & Conversation Access

> **FINAL ANSWER (For Defense):** The Negotiation Hub is fully end-to-end encrypted. Agents CANNOT see any buyer-farmer conversations during normal operations. ONLY during an active dispute, and ONLY after an admin explicitly grants access, can an agent view the conversation history for investigation.

### 📋 SYSTEM OVERVIEW
The Hub connects Buyers and Farmers through E2E Encrypted channels. ZimAgritrust maintains privacy as a default state; neither Agents nor Admins can read chat logs during standard trade.

### 👤 AGENT'S ROLE IN NEGOTIATIONS
* **Normal Mode**: You verified quality/quantity. You DO NOT see or interfere with chat or pricing.
* **Dispute Mode**: If a formal dispute is raised, you may request access to the chat logs. Once granted, you review the history for evidence, promises made, and timestamps.

### 🔒 END-TO-END ENCRYPTION (E2EE)
Messages are encrypted on the sender's device and decrypted only on the recipient's.
* **Access Scope**: If access is granted, it is TEMPORARY (7 days) and restricted to the specific transaction in dispute.
* **Audit Trail**: Every time an agent views a decrypted conversation, the action is logged for accountability.

### 🔓 DISPUTE ACCESS PROTOCOL
1. **Dispute Raised**: Status remains encrypted.
2. **Admin Review**: Admin reviews the dispute reason.
3. **Access Granted**: Admin generates a temporary decryption key for the Agent.
4. **Agent Investigation**: Agent reviews history and media.
5. **Revocation**: Access is automatically revoked after resolution or expiry.

### ⚖️ INVESTIGATION EVIDENCE
During a dispute, you will see a decrypted view of the chat including:
* Exact price offers and acceptances.
* Shared photos of the crop being loaded.
* Promises regarding delivery dates or grade.
* Claims made by both parties.

4.2 Investigation Protocols
--------------------------
All investigations must be objective and evidence-based. You are looking for the 'Point of Truth' based on the immutable ledger and the encrypted chat history.

4.3 Resolution Decision-Making
------------------------------
Once evidence is reviewed, you must recommend a resolution:
* **Refund to Buyer**: If the farmer failed to meet quality/quantity promised in the Hub.
* **Release to Farmer**: If the buyer is making a frivolous claim or if the delivery matches the verified listing.
* **Partial Settlement**: If both parties agree to a compromise based on minor defects.
