ZimAgritrust Module 9: Advanced Ag-Tech & IoT
=============================================

Purpose
-------
This module introduces the technologies that support agent verification, including sensors, imagery, AI tools and immutable ledgers.

9.1 IoT and Smart Sensors
-------------------------
IoT devices collect field and warehouse data in real time.

Examples:
- Bluetooth moisture probes.
- Smart scales.
- Soil nutrient sensors.
- Warehouse temperature and humidity monitors.

If a sensor appears faulty, use a secondary tool and flag the report.

9.2 Drone and Satellite Imagery
-------------------------------
Satellite imagery can verify field size, crop health and vegetation changes.

NDVI maps show plant health and may reveal disease, drought stress or false acreage claims. Agents interpret imagery but do not need to fly drones unless specifically trained.

9.3 AI and Photo Verification
-----------------------------
The platform may use AI to classify crops, detect disease, identify quality risks and flag fraud.

Agent rule:
AI supports your judgement but does not replace physical verification. If AI and field evidence disagree, escalate with photos and notes.

9.4 Blockchain-Inspired Immutability
------------------------------------
Signed reports cannot be silently changed or deleted. Corrections are appended as new records.

Why it matters:
- Buyers trust the evidence.
- Audits can reconstruct events.
- Agent accountability is preserved.

Final Summary
-------------
Technology increases accuracy, but agents remain responsible for field truth. Use tools carefully and document every decision.
