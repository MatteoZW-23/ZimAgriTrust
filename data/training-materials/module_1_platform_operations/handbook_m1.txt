1.1 MISSION: SOVEREIGN TRUST IN AGRICULTURAL TRADE
================================================================================

Our mission is to eliminate the 'trust deficit' that plagues rural agricultural
markets. In most developing regions, farmers lack access to fair markets and
finance because there is no reliable way to verify their yield or quality
without expensive intermediaries.

ZimAgriTrust solves this through:

+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------+
| Solution            | Description                                            |
+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------+
| Sovereign Identity  | Giving every farmer a verified digital twin           |
| Verifiable Proof    | Using field agents (you) to provide ground-truth data |
| Smart Escrow        | Ensuring payment is only released when quality is     |
|                     | confirmed                                              |
+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------+


================================================================================
1.2 THE ZIMAGRITRUST TECH STACK
================================================================================

ZimAgriTrust is a multi-layered infrastructure designed for low-connectivity
environments:

+------------------+------------------------+------------------------------------+

| ML Intelligence  | AI models              | Crop photo analysis                |
+------------------+------------------------+------------------------------------+

1.4 THE 6 STEPS OF A ZIMAGRITRUST TRADE
================================================================================

STEP 1: LISTING
---------------
Farmer lists crops via WhatsApp or USSD
-> System creates digital record of offer
-> Agent receives notification

STEP 2: COMMITMENT
------------------
Buyer deposits 100% of value into Sovereign Escrow Pool
-> Funds frozen in trust account
-> Transaction secured

STEP 3: VERIFICATION (YOUR ROLE)
--------------------------------
Agent receives notification and travels to farm
-> Physical inspection performed
-> Evidence collected
-> Report submitted

STEP 4: GRADING (YOUR ROLE)
---------------------------
Agent assigns Grade based on:
-> Moisture content
-> Purity percentage
-> Visual quality assessment

STEP 5: DELIVERY
----------------
Crop is moved to certified warehouse
-> Chain of custody recorded
-> Delivery confirmed

STEP 6: SETTLEMENT
------------------
Escrow system automatically releases funds
-> Platform fee deducted
-> Farmer paid
-> Trust scores updated

FINAL SUMMARY
================================================================================

Field Agents are the 'GROUND TRUTH' for ZimAgriTrust. You are responsible for
ensuring that the digital records in our cloud match the physical reality on
the farm.

YOUR CORE RESPONSIBILITIES:
1. Verify listings through physical farm visits
2. Witness deliveries and confirm matches
3. Resolve disputes by reviewing evidence and interviewing parties
4. Support farmers with platform usage
5. Maintain integrity through accurate reporting

YOUR TOOLS:
- Agent Portal (web-based dashboard)
- WhatsApp AI Assistant (24/7 support)
- Mobile phone with camera

YOUR EARNINGS:
- Verification fees ($0.50 - $4.50 per listing)
- Dispute resolution fees ($1.00 - $6.50 per dispute)
- Weekly performance bonuses (up to $60/week)
- Referral bonuses ($5 per new active farmer)

By mastering the platform operations, adhering to the code of conduct, and
leveraging your WhatsApp AI assistant, you ensure the success and integrity of
Zimbabwe's agricultural marketplace.
