ZimAgritrust Module 8: Legal & Regulatory Framework
===================================================

Purpose
-------
This module explains the legal responsibilities of agents when verifying crops, land, identity, escrow activity and digital signatures.

8.1 Trust Deed and Platform Terms
---------------------------------
Every user participates under the platform trust framework. Agent reports form part of the transaction evidence record.

Important principles:
- Escrow release becomes final after delivery confirmation.
- Agent digital signature acts as witness evidence.
- Disputes follow platform arbitration before escalation.

8.2 Land Tenure and Rights
--------------------------
Agents may verify that crops are connected to legitimate farming rights.

Common documents:
- Title deed: formal ownership.
- Leasehold: right to use land for a defined period.
- Communal allotment: traditional or local authority confirmation.

Always capture document references only through secure platform workflows.

8.3 RBZ and Financial Compliance
--------------------------------
ZimAgritrust operates in a regulated financial environment.

Compliance rules:
- Escrow and operating funds must not be mixed.
- KYC is required for traders.
- Suspicious high-value transactions must be flagged.
- Audit trails must be preserved.

8.4 Agent Legal Risk
--------------------
False reports, bribery, data leaks, or intentional misrepresentation may expose the agent to disciplinary and legal action.

Final Summary
-------------
Agents are operational witnesses. Your reports must be truthful, compliant, and supported by evidence.
