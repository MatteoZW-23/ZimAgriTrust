ZimAgritrust Module 7: Rural Finance & Credit Access
===================================================

Purpose
-------
This module teaches agents how verified agricultural data helps smallholder farmers access credit, insurance, and financial services.

7.1 Inventory-Backed Lending
----------------------------
Farmers often have crops but no cash. Verified inventory can become collateral.

Example flow:
1. Agent verifies 10 tonnes of maize.
2. Crop is sealed, tagged and logged.
3. Bank issues a crop-backed loan.
4. When buyer pays, loan is settled first.
5. Remaining funds go to the farmer.

Agent responsibility:
- Report quantity and quality accurately.
- Never exaggerate collateral value.
- Capture clear photos and GPS evidence.
- Understand that false reporting can cause financial loss.

7.2 Credit Scoring for Smallholders
-----------------------------------
Traditional banks rely on payslips and formal employment records. ZimAgritrust uses verified agricultural performance.

Data points:
- Yield growth over seasons.
- Repayment and delivery reliability.
- Quality consistency.
- Dispute history.
- Timely listing and fulfilment.

7.3 Insurance & Risk
--------------------
Insurance protects farmers from drought, pests and crop failure.

Parametric insurance may trigger payouts automatically when satellite or weather data meets set conditions. Agents may be sent to verify field conditions for claims.

7.4 Agent Conduct in Finance Tasks
----------------------------------
Loan and insurance verification must be extra careful because money is released based on your evidence.

Rules:
- Visit the farm physically.
- Capture GPS and photos.
- Do not approve claims without evidence.
- Escalate suspicious claims.

Final Summary
-------------
Accurate verification gives farmers access to finance while protecting banks, insurers and the platform from fraud.
