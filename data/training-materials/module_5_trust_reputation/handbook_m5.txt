ZimAgritrust Module 5: Trust & Reputation System
================================================

Purpose
-------
This module explains how agent behaviour, accuracy, timeliness, and user feedback affect reputation and work eligibility.

5.1 Trust Score Algorithm
-------------------------
The trust score is a live measure of professional reliability. It affects task assignment, audit priority, suspension risk, and promotion eligibility.

Typical weighting:
- Grading accuracy: 40%
- Response time: 30%
- User feedback: 20%
- System integrity: 10%

Critical thresholds:
- Below 70/100: temporary suspension or review.
- Below 40/100: permanent ban or serious disciplinary action.

5.2 Review & Rating Management
------------------------------
Farmers and buyers may rate agent professionalism after transactions. Good ratings come from punctuality, respectful communication, transparent evidence, and clear explanations.

Best practice:
- Explain the grade with visible readings.
- Send updates if delayed.
- Help farmers understand next actions.
- Keep evidence for bad-faith complaints.

5.3 Career Progression Levels
-----------------------------
ZimAgritrust field work is a career path.

Levels:
- Field Trainee: supervised verification only.
- Certified Agent: independent standard verifications.
- Senior Agent: high-value listings, audits, MFI loan verification.
- Lead Auditor: dispute reviews and agent quality investigations.

5.4 Audit and Appeals
---------------------
If a rating or audit is disputed, the agent must submit GPS logs, timestamped photos, readings, chat references where permitted, and supervisor notes.

Final Summary
-------------
Your reputation is built through accuracy, speed, fairness, and evidence. High trust unlocks better tasks and leadership opportunities.
