ZimAgritrust Module 3: Crop Verification & Quality Standards
=============================================================

Purpose
-------
This module trains agents to become reliable ground-truth verifiers for crop quality, quantity, disease risk, and batch integrity.

3.1 Crop Grading Standards
--------------------------
Agents must apply consistent grading rules across all districts. The agent grade becomes the platform's field truth and affects escrow release, buyer confidence, farmer trust score, and future finance access.

Maize grading reference:
- Grade A: moisture below 12.5%, broken grains below 2%, foreign matter below 0.5%, no infestation.
- Grade B: moisture 12.5-14%, broken grains 2-5%, foreign matter 0.5-1.5%, no active infestation.
- Grade C: moisture 14.1-15.5%, broken grains 5-10%, foreign matter 1.5-3%, trace infestation only.
- Reject: aflatoxin signs, chemical contamination, internal heat, severe infestation, or unsafe storage condition.

3.2 Sampling Techniques
-----------------------
Representative sampling prevents fraud and protects all parties.

Use the 5-point probe method:
1. Probe bottom-left to base.
2. Probe top-right diagonally.
3. Take centre core sample.
4. Probe opposite corners.
5. Mix samples in a clean bucket and test from the composite.

Never sample only the top layer because poor-quality grain may be hidden underneath.

3.3 Visual Disease Detection
----------------------------
Agents use visual inspection first, then the app AI lens for confirmation.

Common risks:
- Aflatoxin: yellow-green mold; quarantine immediately.
- Maize lethal necrosis: streaked leaves and shriveled cobs.
- Large grain borer: visible holes and powder/flour in bags.

If disease is suspected, take at least three clear macro photos and flag for senior audit.

3.4 Verification Hardware
-------------------------
The verification kit may include a digital moisture meter, Bluetooth platform scale, phone/tablet camera, GPS geotagger, and QR batch tools.

Operational rules:
- Calibrate moisture meters every 30 days.
- Sync scale readings directly where possible.
- Confirm GPS before submitting field reports.
- Low battery can cause inaccurate readings; check device health before work.

3.5 Batch Tagging & Sealing
---------------------------
Once verified, the crop batch must be protected from tampering.

Process:
1. Generate a unique batch ID.
2. Attach QR/tamper-evident tag.
3. Photograph the seal on the actual bags.
4. Record quantity, grade, GPS and timestamp.
5. Driver scans QR code during handover.

Final Summary
-------------
Accurate crop verification protects buyers, rewards honest farmers, and makes the ZimAgritrust ledger credible. Verify with evidence, not assumptions.
