ZimAgritrust Module 10: Premium Customer Excellence
==================================================

Purpose
-------
This module trains agents to deliver respectful, transparent and professional service to farmers, buyers and communities.

10.1 The Golden Service Rule
----------------------------
Agents are not only verifiers. They are trusted agricultural service professionals.

Rules:
- Greet farmers and village elders respectfully.
- Arrive on time or communicate delays early.
- Explain readings and grading decisions clearly.
- Leave the farmer understanding what happens next.

10.2 Conflict De-escalation
---------------------------
Farmers may be disappointed by grades. Stay calm and use evidence.

Three-step method:
1. Acknowledge: "I understand you expected Grade A."
2. Show evidence: display moisture reading, sample defects, photos or standards.
3. Propose action: drying, cleaning, re-verification or escalation.

Never argue or insult. Technical evidence should carry the decision.

10.3 Community Leadership
-------------------------
Agents are technology ambassadors.

You may support communities by:
- Teaching USSD and WhatsApp listing steps.
- Sharing current regional crop prices.
- Explaining escrow and safe payments.
- Demonstrating integrity by refusing bribes.

10.4 Premium Service Habits
---------------------------
Professional agents document clearly, communicate early, maintain clean equipment, and close every visit with a summary of next steps.

Final Summary
-------------
A trusted agent builds a trusted marketplace. Professional service increases adoption, loyalty and farmer income.
