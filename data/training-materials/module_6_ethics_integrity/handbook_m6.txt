ZimAgritrust Module 6: Marketplace Ethics & Integrity
=====================================================

Purpose
-------
This module protects the marketplace from corruption, data misuse, conflict of interest, and off-platform manipulation.

6.1 Professional Ethics
-----------------------
ZimAgritrust depends on trusted field truth. Corruption destroys the ledger and harms farmers.

Zero tolerance policies:
- No bribery: accepting gifts or payments to influence grading leads to dismissal.
- No collusion: inflating grades or hiding defects is fraud.
- No poaching: never encourage users to trade off-platform to avoid fees.
- No false reporting: every report must match physical evidence.

6.2 Data Privacy & Security
---------------------------
Agents handle sensitive data including GPS coordinates, national IDs, farm photos, financial information, phone numbers, and earnings records.

Rules:
- Share data only inside approved platform workflows.
- Never post farmer or warehouse photos on social media.
- Keep devices PIN-protected.
- Do not export or screenshot private user data unless required by workflow.
- Encrypted chats may only be accessed during approved dispute investigations.

6.3 Conflict of Interest
------------------------
Agents must remain neutral.

Examples:
- Do not verify crops owned by relatives without disclosure.
- Do not buy crops you previously verified.
- Disclose employment or commercial ties with other ag-tech firms.
- Recuse yourself if neutrality could be questioned.

6.4 Enforcement
---------------
Serious misconduct may be recorded in the audit ledger, shared with governance teams, trigger suspension, or result in permanent removal.

Final Summary
-------------
Integrity is not optional. Agents are trusted because they cannot be bought, pressured, or biased.
