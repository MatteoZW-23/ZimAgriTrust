2.1 Clarified Transaction Flow and Escrow Storage
-------------------------------------------------

# 💰 ESCROW & PAYMENT HOLDING - PHYSICALLY & LEGALLY

The platform holds money from the BUYER on behalf of the transaction. Funds are held in ESCROW until both parties confirm delivery.

### 🏛️ WHERE IS THE MONEY PHYSICALLY HELD?

The escrow money is NOT stored in the company's operating bank account. It is held in separate, dedicated **Trust Accounts** at licensed financial institutions in Zimbabwe.

*   **USD Transactions**: Held in a dedicated **USD Trust Account** at partner banks (NMB, Agribank, or CBZ).
*   **ZiG Transactions**: Held in a dedicated **ZiG Trust Account** at the same partner banks.
*   **EcoCash Transactions**: Held in a **segregated EcoCash Merchant Escrow Wallet**.

**LEGAL PRINCIPLE:** The platform never "touches" the money for business operations. The money moves directly from buyer to escrow to seller, never passing through our personal or corporate operational accounts.

### ⏱️ THE 4-STAGE MONEY MOVEMENT

| Stage | Activity | Money Location |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **1. Payment** | Buyer pays for a product offer. | Moves from Buyer Wallet to **Escrow Trust Account**. |
| **2. Holding** | Seller prepares and ships the product. | **FROZEN** in the Trust Account (Platform acts as Trustee). |
| **3. Delivery** | Product arrives and is inspected. | Still in **Escrow Holding**. (Protects buyer from loss). |
| **4. Release** | Buyer clicks "Confirm Delivery". | Moves from Trust Account to **Seller Virtual Wallet**. |

### 🏦 THE TRUST ACCOUNT ADVANTAGE
In Zimbabwe, holding money for third parties (escrow) requires specific legal arrangements. 
1. **Bankruptcy Protection**: If the company fails, your money in the Trust Account is legally protected and cannot be touched by creditors.
2. **RBZ Compliance**: We report to the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe to prevent fraud and money laundering.
3. **No Commingling**: We never mix escrow funds with operating funds. Fees for agents are paid from the 1% platform commission, not from the trade principal.

---

### 💵 PART 2: THE WITHDRAWAL PROCESS
When a seller or agent "withdraws" money from their virtual wallet:
1. The system checks the virtual ledger balance.
2. The system instructs the bank or EcoCash to transfer REAL money from the pooled Trust Account to the user's personal account.
3. The money officially leaves the AgriTrust ecosystem.
