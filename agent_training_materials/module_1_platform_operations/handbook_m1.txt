AgriTrust Module 1: Platform Operations (Core)
========================================

Welcome to the AgriTrust Academy. This handbook provides the core operational protocols required for all Field Agents.

1.1 Mission: Sovereign Trust in Agricultural Trade
--------------------------------------------------
Our mission is to eliminate the 'trust deficit' that plagues rural agricultural markets. In most developing regions, farmers lack access to fair markets and finance because there is no reliable way to verify their yield or quality without expensive intermediaries.

AgriTrust solves this through:
* Sovereign Identity: Giving every farmer a verified digital twin.
* Verifiable Proof: Using field agents (you) to provide ground-truth data.
* Smart Escrow: Ensuring payment is only released when quality is confirmed.

1.2 The AgriTrust Tech Stack
----------------------------
AgriTrust is a multi-layered infrastructure designed for low-connectivity environments:
1. Core Backend: A FastAPI-driven engine that handles the ledger, escrow, and user management.
2. Agent Portal (Your Interface): A high-performance web application designed for mobile devices in the field.
3. Farmer Interface: Primarily USSD and WhatsApp-based.
4. IoT Gateway: Automated sensors in warehouses.
5. ML Intelligence: AI models that analyze crop photos.

1.3 User Roles & Responsibilities
---------------------------------
* Farmers: The producers. They list crops and request verification.
* Agents (You): The Verifiers. You are the ONLY source of truth for the digital system.
* Buyers: Wholesalers who purchase listed crops.
* Administrators: Global monitors who handle disputes.

1.4 The 6 Steps of an AgriTrust Trade
-------------------------------------
1. Listing: Farmer lists crops via WhatsApp.
2. Commitment: Buyer deposits 100% of the value into the Sovereign Escrow Pool.
3. Verification: Agent receive a notification and travels to the farm.
4. Grading: Agent assigns a Grade based on moisture and purity.
5. Delivery: The crop is moved to a certified warehouse.
6. Settlement: Escrow System automatically releases funds.

1.5 Platform Policies
---------------------
* Zero Tolerance for Bribery: Accepting gifts results in immediate permanent ban.
* Data Privacy: Farmer contact details must never be shared outside the platform.
* Timeliness: Verification requests must be actioned within 24 hours.
* Accuracy: Intentional misreporting of weight is considered fraud.

1.6 Core Agent Functions (What you actually do)
----------------------------------------------
### 🎯 CORE FUNCTIONS
1. VERIFY LISTINGS: Visit farms, confirm crops, check quantity/quality, and submit reports.
2. CONFIRM DELIVERIES: Witness handovers, verify matches, and mark as delivered.
3. RESOLVE DISPUTES: Review evidence, interview parties, and recommend resolutions.
4. SUPPORT FARMERS: Help with registration, USSD usage, and listing creation.
5. VERIFY LOANS: Confirm identity and assesses farm viability for financial services.

### 📊 QUICK SUMMARY
* Listing Verification: 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY
* Delivery Confirmation: 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY
* Dispute Resolution: 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY
* Farmer Support: 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY
* Loan Verification: 🟢 LOW PRIORITY

1.7 WhatsApp AI Agent Assistant
-------------------------------

# 🤖 WHATSAPP BOT AS AN AI AGENT – Balancing All Duties

The WhatsApp bot is **ONE AI agent** that performs **multiple roles simultaneously**. It is your 24/7 technical partner in the field.

### 📋 THE AI ASSISTANT CONCEPT
The bot acts as a support system for Farmers, Buyers, Admins, and most importantly, YOU (The Agent). It handles routine data collection and alerts, allowing you to focus on physical verification and judgment.

### 🎯 HOW THE BOT SUPPORTS YOU
| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Task Assignment** | Alerts you instantly: "New verification task assigned to you." |
| **Field Guidance** | Provides checklists: "Step 1: Take crop photo. Step 2: Verify quantity..." |
| **Evidence Hub** | Collects photos/docs from parties during a dispute to save you time. |
| **Earnings Tracking** | View your commissions instantly by asking the bot. |
| **Navigation** | Send 'location' to get directions to a registered farm. |

### 🧠 INTENT DETECTION
The bot uses AI to understand user messages across Shona, Ndebele, and English. It can handle a Farmer's price check, a Buyer's search, and your task query all at the same time without mixing up the conversations.

### ✅ WHAT THE BOT DOES VS WHAT YOU DO
* **WhatsApp Bot**: Handles 80% of routine inquiries, price checks, and data entry.
* **Human Agent (You)**: Handles the physical reality—touching the grain, witnessing the delivery, and making the final decision in complex disputes.
